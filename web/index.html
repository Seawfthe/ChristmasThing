How the night flows

Four steps, no clipboard

Sign up

Fill in the registration form. A row appears in attendees.xlsx instantly, and you get a ticket code.

Get your QR

Your ticket page renders a QR code. Screenshot it, add it to your wallet, or keep the tab open.

Scan at the door

The desk scans you in. Status flips to Checked in, the timestamp fills itself, the chime plays.

Win something

At 21:30 the draw reads the checked-in names, picks one at random, and logs it on the Winners sheet.

Under the hood

One Excel file is the whole database

No accounts, no cloud, no monthly bill. The app reads and writes data/attendees.xlsx live — you can open it, fix a typo, add a column, and the site picks it up on the next read.
