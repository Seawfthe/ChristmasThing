How the night flows

Four steps, no clipboard

Sign up

Fill in the registration form. A row appears in the guest list instantly, and you get a ticket code.

Get your QR

Your ticket page renders a QR code. Screenshot it, add it to your wallet, or keep the tab open.

Scan at the door

The desk scans you in. Status flips to Checked in, the timestamp fills itself, the chime plays.

Win something

At 21:30 the draw reads the checked-in names, picks one at random, and logs it on the Winners sheet.

Under the hood

One Excel file is the whole database

This build runs entirely in the browser: the guest list lives in this browser's storage and becomes a real attendees.xlsx whenever you download it. Open it in Excel, fix a typo, add a column, then import it back — no server, no accounts, no monthly bill.
